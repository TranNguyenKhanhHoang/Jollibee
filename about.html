Who we are

NovaCart is a fictional e-commerce and digital retail platform company created for our COS10026 student project. Our concept focuses on making online shopping more convenient, accessible and customer-friendly.

What We Do

  • Explore e-commerce platform solutions.
  • Improve digital shopping experiences.
  • Explore customer experience technology.
  • Help businesses connect with online customers.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To make online retail experiences simple, accessible and enjoyable for businesses and customers.

Our vision

To create a digital marketplace where technology helps people discover products and connect with businesses easily.
