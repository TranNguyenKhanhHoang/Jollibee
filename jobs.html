Application Tips

Stand out from the crowd! Make sure your CV includes:

  • Links to your GitHub repository and live web projects.
  • Demonstrated knowledge of WCAG 2.1 web accessibility.
  • Clear examples of responsive layout development.

Quick Checklist

Before submitting your application, double-check that you have:

  • Updated contact details & LinkedIn profile.
  • PDF format resume (under 5MB).
  • Cover letter tailored to NovaCart values.
